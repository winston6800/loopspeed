The problem

Every property has its own water, power, gas, trash, and vendor accounts. Someone on your team has to chase all of them, every month.

Too many portals

Dozens of logins across utilities and vendors, each with its own layout, password rules, and billing cycle.

Manual data entry

Every bill gets downloaded, read, coded to the right property and GL account, and typed into your accounting system by hand.

Missed and late bills

One forgotten portal means late fees, shutoff notices, and a messy month end close.

How it works

Three steps. You stay in control of what gets posted.

1

Connect your portals

Add the utility and vendor accounts for each property once.

2

The agent pulls and codes bills

It logs in on schedule, downloads new bills, and codes each one by property and GL account.

3

Review and push

Approve the batch, then send it to AppFolio or QuickBooks in one click.

Who it is for

Small and mid-size property managers, roughly 50 to 2,000 units, who are too small for enterprise bill pay platforms and too big to keep doing this by hand.

  • Residential and mixed portfolios with many separate utility accounts
  • Teams running on AppFolio or QuickBooks
  • Bookkeepers and property accountants who spend days each month on bill entry
